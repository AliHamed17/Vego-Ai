# EXP-040 — Thesis claim-readiness audit

## Question

Which thesis claims are supported now, and which still require independent
labels, human studies, or external replication?

## Method

Map the paper baseline, thesis research questions, hypotheses, experiment
results, and claim gates into three evidence lanes:

1. demonstrated mechanism/capability;
2. contextual historical evidence;
3. not-yet-measurable empirical value.

## Acceptance

- Every claim has an experiment, metric or gate, and evidence class.
- Null and negative results remain visible.
- Classification improvement, generalization, and effort claims remain closed
  until their predefined gates pass.

## Claim boundary

This audit evaluates evidence readiness and traceability, not the substantive
truth of currently unmeasured hypotheses.
