## Question

Is the exact August supervisor package complete, evidence-bounded, readable,
rehearsed, versioned, safely delivered, and independently accessible to Iris
and Arnon, with every current control reachable from the presentation?

## Inputs

- Candidate English 12-slide PPTX and PDF.
- Presenter notes and reviewed bilingual evidence appendix.
- `docs/research/meetings/2026-08-05-supervisor-presentation-manifest.md`
- `docs/research/meetings/2026-08-05-supervisor-presentation-checklist.md`
- `docs/research/meetings/2026-08-05-supervisor-rehearsal-record.md`
- `docs/research/meetings/2026-08-05-supervisor-delivery-access-record.md`
- Current master traceability, decision, claim, and external-fact registers.
- Current RQ decision pack, three-study contract, Plan A/B comparison, and
  citation/source manifest.

## Procedure

1. Freeze the candidate PPTX, PDF, notes, appendix, and manifests; record
   versions and hashes before review.
2. Require the 12 core topics in the approved order and verify that every
   current R/A/Q or newly discovered control is directly shown or reachable in
   the appendix through the presentation manifest.
3. Map every material slide statement to a claim state, evidence source,
   limitation, and presenter note. Fail unreviewed quotations or unsupported
   accuracy, effort, medical, partner, deadline, access, or approval language.
4. Render every slide and inspect it for overflow, clipping, contrast, font
   substitution, Hebrew right-to-left errors, citation readability, and broken
   tables or links.
5. Run one timed live rehearsal and one adversarial question-and-answer
   rehearsal using the exact frozen candidate. Record issues, correct mandatory
   findings, re-render, and rerun the complete preflight.
6. Obtain Ali's explicit review of the exact final hashes before sharing.
7. Share only the approved package. Iris and Arnon independently open the PPTX
   or PDF and every required link; record recipient, permission, time, version,
   hash, result, and correction without exposing restricted data.
8. Preserve an offline fallback copy and the exact delivered package.

## Outputs

- Frozen presentation package and hash manifest.
- Per-control slide/appendix reachability matrix.
- Rendered-slide QA and correction report.
- Timed and adversarial rehearsal record.
- Ali review evidence and recipient delivery/access record.

acceptance requires all targets and a second clean preflight after mandatory
revisions. A successful local build is not delivery; a sent message is not
recipient access; rehearsal outcomes are not real supervisor decisions.
