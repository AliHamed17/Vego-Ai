AI Review Loop — Claim Validation Process
How every thesis claim is checked against 18 evidence-consistency invariants and evidence gates before publication.
① DRAFT CLAIM
Thesis writes a claim about artifact behavior or results. E.g., "The selective policy targets 40.7% of patterns."
② 18 INVARIANT CHECKS
Automated CI verifies consistency with code + data. Cross-refs, figure refs, citation integrity, terminology, running example thread, claim language guard.
③ EVIDENCE GATE
Claim checked against label count. If 0 labels → bounded. If <20 → pilot only. Accuracy claims blocked until ≥20 generalization-safe labels.
④ ACCEPT or REVISE
Claim either accepted with appropriate bounds stated, or sent back for revision. Feedback loop until all checks pass.
