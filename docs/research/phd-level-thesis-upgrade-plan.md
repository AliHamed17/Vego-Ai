## What makes a design-science thesis "PhD level"
1. A **theoretical contribution**, not only an artifact — a *nascent design theory* (Gregor & Hevner, 2013):
   constructs, principles of form and function, testable propositions, justificatory (kernel) knowledge.
2. **Critical synthesis** of literature (positions engaged and contrasted), not a catalogue.
3. **Generalization** argued and designed for (transfer across domains/diagrams/reviewers/models).
4. **Methodological rigor** that is itself a contribution (pre-registration, leakage discipline, gates).
5. **Reflexivity** — explicit boundary conditions, researcher bias, and what would falsify the claims.
