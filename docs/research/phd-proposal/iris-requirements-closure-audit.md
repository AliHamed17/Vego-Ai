## How this audit closes

A row moves only after its stated gate is evidenced. Supervisor silence is `Deferred`, not acceptance. Within 24 hours after each supervisor meeting:

1. record decisions and corrections in the [decision/change log](./decision-change-log.md);
2. update the master register and this audit;
3. propagate exact RQ, plan, claim, schedule, and ownership changes to every affected artifact;
4. retain the raw media and ASR unchanged;
5. rerun `python scripts/validate_iris_requirements_closure.py --all --refresh`; and
6. archive the exact presented package only after Ali reviews and authorizes it.
