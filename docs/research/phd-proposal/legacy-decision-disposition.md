## Operating rule

- `Carried forward` means the unresolved gate still blocks the relevant claim
  or implementation.
- `Absorbed` means the topic remains as a construct, hypothesis, or study
  parameter; it is not an extra active top-level decision.
- `Superseded` means successor wording controls new documents, while the
  historical record remains unchanged.
- Silence, calendar attendance, document creation, or code availability never
  counts as supervisor approval.
