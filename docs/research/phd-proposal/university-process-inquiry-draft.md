## Draft subject

Request for authoritative PhD proposal and candidacy-process requirements

## Draft message

Dear [authorized department or Graduate Studies contact],

I am preparing my PhD proposal in the Information Systems program and would
like to verify the current formal proposal and candidacy requirements that
apply to my case. Please confirm the following points or direct me to the
authoritative current policy and responsible office.

1. What is the official proposal or candidacy submission deadline, including
   the date, time, timezone, and rule for weekends or university closures?
2. How many reviewers or examiners are required, and what eligibility,
   independence, conflict-of-interest, rank, or institutional-affiliation rules
   apply?
3. What is the reviewer nomination, approval, invitation, and replacement
   process? Who initiates each step, and which forms or approvals are required?
4. How is the committee composed and appointed? Who chairs or approves it, and
   are there required internal or external members?
5. Which forms, cover pages, declarations, signatures, supervisor approvals,
   ethics/data-management attachments, and administrative materials are
   mandatory?
6. What is the authorized submission channel, file format, naming convention,
   and evidence of receipt? Is email, an online system, or another route
   required?
7. What proposal length, structure, formatting, language, abstract, and
   reference-style requirements apply? Is an official template mandatory?
8. Is a candidacy presentation or examination required? If so, what are its
   duration, audience, format, assessment criteria, language, and required
   presentation materials?
9. What lead times apply between proposal submission, administrative check,
   reviewer appointment, reviewer reading, committee scheduling, and the
   presentation or examination?
10. Is a preliminary-results section mandatory, recommended, or optional at
    this stage? Are there minimum methodological or progress expectations?
11. What correction, resubmission, amendment, or appeal process applies after
    supervisor, reviewer, committee, or administrative feedback?
12. Are there program-specific or student-status-specific rules that change any
    of the above requirements for my case?

Please provide the current policy title, version or effective date, stable URL
or attached document, issuing office, and a contact for any follow-up. If more
than one authority governs the process, please identify which source controls
each requirement.

Thank you,

Ali Hamed
