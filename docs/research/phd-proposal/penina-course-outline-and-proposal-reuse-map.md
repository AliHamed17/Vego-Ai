## Presentation outline

1. Problem: one-off expert corrections do not become governed reusable
   judgment.
2. Structured-norm assessment and why valid variability complicates automatic
   evaluation.
3. Agentic and multi-agent human-oversight architectures.
4. Selective intervention: uncertainty, importance, workload, and escalation.
5. Representing expert judgment: provenance, scope, rationale, confidence,
   conflict, adjudication, expiry, and revocation.
6. Safe reuse: retrieval, transparency, authority, verification, and unsafe
   generalization.
7. Evaluation: quality, consistency, traceability, expert effort, reliability,
   robustness, and validity threats.
8. Software/modeling evidence and the VEGO-AI baseline.
9. Conditional medical transfer: CDSS override/alert fatigue, clinical
   guidelines, healthcare conformance, and governance boundaries.
10. Literature gaps, contradictions, and the three-study doctoral program.
11. Plan A/Plan B feasibility and open research decisions.
