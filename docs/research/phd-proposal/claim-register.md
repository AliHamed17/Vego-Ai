## Evidence-state vocabulary

| State | Meaning | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Established | Directly inspectable fact supported by current artifacts, records, or verified process evidence. It does not imply effectiveness beyond the stated fact. | May be stated as a current fact with a source. |
| Preliminary | Relevant evidence exists, but scope, independence, sample size, validation, or claim strength is insufficient for a definitive conclusion. | Must use qualifiers such as “preliminary,” “in the current software/modeling setting,” and explicit limitations. |
| Planned | A proposed study, artifact, or evaluation has a documented design but has not produced the required evidence. | Use future tense only; do not imply approval, execution, or outcome. |
| Blocked | A required evidence, authorization, or human gate is unsatisfied. Execution or positive claim is prohibited. | State the blocker and required gate; never report a positive result. |
| Partner-dependent | Delivery depends on an external organization, named experts, data, permissions, infrastructure, or schedule that is not controlled or fully evidenced by the project. | State the dependency and fallback; do not imply commitment or access. |

## Release check

Before sharing any proposal, deck, report, or partner brief:

1. search for every quantitative, comparative, medical, partner, deadline, and novelty statement;
2. map it to a claim ID;
3. use only the permitted wording or a stricter formulation;
4. cite the exact current evidence;
5. state the relevant limitation or dependency; and
6. block release if an unregistered or over-strength claim remains.
