# Independent Reviewer Participant Information and Consent

Study: VEGO-AI independent evidence evaluation.

Stage: reviewer calibration, followed by blinded evaluation only after the
calibration instructions are frozen by a human.

## Purpose

The study asks whether VEGO-AI classifications and review-routing decisions
agree with judgments made independently by qualified human reviewers. Your
judgments are the reference evidence; VEGO-AI output is deliberately hidden.

Participation does not test you personally. The research evaluates the system,
its classification categories, and its routing logic.

## What you will do

1. Complete three calibration patterns.
2. Record one classification, rationale, confidence level, human-review need,
   routing rationale, and review priority for each pattern.
3. Work independently without consulting VEGO-AI, Human Judgment Memory,
   generative AI, or the other reviewer.
4. If the instructions are human-frozen after calibration, complete a separate
   24-pattern blinded evaluation using the same fields.

The page also records active time while it is visible. It stores progress only
in your browser and makes local JSON/CSV exports; it sends no network request.

## Data collected

- A stable pseudonymous reviewer ID, not your name or email address.
- Classification, rationale, confidence, routing judgment, priority, notes,
  review date, and active seconds.
- No VEGO-AI prediction, memory advice, private split, or other reviewer's
  answer is shown to you.

The project owner may know your real identity through the private recruitment
channel, but the research files use only the pseudonymous ID.

## Storage, transfer, and retention

- Return files are transferred one-to-one through institutional email or an
  access-controlled drive.
- Raw returns are stored only in the ignored local research-evidence directory.
- Raw returns are preserved unchanged; corrections are recorded separately.
- Data is retained through thesis acceptance plus 12 months and then deleted,
  subject to any applicable institutional requirement.
- Raw reviewer files, identities, and private mappings are not committed to
  GitHub or placed in the public BigUI.

## Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may stop before exporting a return. You may ask
the project owner, through the same private recruitment channel, to withdraw
your identifiable return before the gold-label set is frozen. After
pseudonymized results are aggregated into the frozen research record, removal
may no longer be technically possible without invalidating the audit trail.

## Risks and benefits

The expected risk is limited to the time required and possible discomfort when
making or explaining a professional judgment. You may use
`Undetermined / Needs Review` and `Insufficient context` when the supplied
context is insufficient.

There is no guaranteed personal benefit. The study may improve understanding
of where human judgment is needed in AI-assisted domain-model assessment.

## Ethics boundary

This document records the project's consent and data-handling procedure. It
does not claim an ethics or IRB approval or waiver. Any applicable institutional
requirement continues to apply.

## Consent confirmation

Before exporting a calibration return, confirm all of the following:

- I have read and understood this information.
- I understand that participation is voluntary.
- I understand what data will be collected and how it will be handled.
- I agree to complete the review independently without consulting the system,
  generative AI, or the other reviewer.
- I consent to the use of my pseudonymous review for the research described
  above.

Record affirmative consent through the private recruitment channel and select
the confirmation checkbox in the offline reviewer page.
